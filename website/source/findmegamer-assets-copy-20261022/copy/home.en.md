## Capabilities

### Start with the game

Bring a Steam game or a clear game brief. Give the research a direction: genre, mechanics, tone, and the kind of coverage you’re looking for.

### Find relevant creators

Research gaming creators across YouTube, Twitch, and X. Focus on relevant content and explainable matches, not audience size alone.

### Understand the fit

Keep matching reasons alongside the evidence available for review. Let your team decide which creators belong on the shortlist.

### Look for public contact details

Enrich a shortlist with available public business contact details. Keep uncertainty visible when information cannot be found or independently verified.

### Prepare outreach with intent

Choose the sending mailbox and template, review the message, and approve the recipients before sending. Installation is never permission to contact someone.

### Keep track of delivery

Inspect task progress, delivery issues, and mailbox monitoring through the workflow. A message accepted by a mail server is not a guarantee of inbox placement or a reply.

## FAQ

**Is this a download or a web app?**

The primary entry point is a prompt for your Agent. It follows the setup guide to install the FindMeGamer CLI and companion Skills. The website introduces the product; it is not the research workspace.

**Can I use it immediately after installation?**

You need an FMG access token and the appropriate permissions. If you do not have one, contact your FMG administrator. Installation alone does not grant service access.

**Which platforms can I research?**

The current workflow includes YouTube, Twitch, and X creator research, with Steam for game context. Available operations depend on your service permissions and provider availability. Platform names do not imply endorsement or partnership.

**Will it find an email for every creator?**

No. Public business contact enrichment is best-effort. No result may be a valid outcome, and a discovered address is not a guarantee of identity, deliverability, or permission to contact its owner.

**Will my Agent send emails automatically?**

Not as part of setup. Outreach requires a separate, approved workflow with the selected mailbox, recipients, and message content.

**Does a successful send mean the message reached the inbox?**

No. Server acceptance, delivery, inbox placement, and a reply are different outcomes. FindMeGamer does not guarantee coverage or replies.

**Are all Agent hosts and operating systems supported?**

The current guide targets Codex with terminal/file tools on macOS or Linux. Compatibility with other hosts must be checked. Do not assume native Windows support or universal Agent integration.

**Is research free?**

Installation does not authorize paid work. Research and enrichment may consume service or provider quota. Confirm access and usage terms with your administrator before starting; no public pricing is specified here.
